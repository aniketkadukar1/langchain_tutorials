FlexFit Gym – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Below are the most common questions our members ask, along with clear answers. If your question isn't listed here, feel free to reach out to the front desk or a trainer anytime!

1. What are the gym’s operating hours?

- Weekdays: 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM  
- Weekends: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
- Closed on national holidays (notifications sent via email and app).

2. Is there a trial or guest pass?

Yes. We offer a 3-day trial pass for first-time users. Guests can also be invited by current members for ₹199/day. Government ID is required.

3. What types of memberships do you offer?

We offer monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and yearly memberships. Special packages are available for couples, students, and corporate groups.

4. Can I freeze or pause my membership?

Yes. You can freeze your membership for up to 30 days once per year due to travel, health, or personal reasons. Submit a request at the front desk or on the app.

5. Do I need to book classes in advance?

Yes. Group classes like Zumba, Yoga, and HIIT require prior booking due to limited slots. Bookings open 24 hours in advance on the app.

6. Is personal training available?

Absolutely. We offer personal training packages with certified coaches. Sessions can be scheduled based on your goals and availability.

7. What if I don’t know how to use a machine?

Our floor trainers are always available during peak hours to assist. You can also scan QR codes on machines to watch short how-to videos.

8. Do you have separate areas for men and women?

No, all workout areas are unisex. However, we do have women-only group fitness sessions and changing rooms for both genders.

9. Is the gym air-conditioned?

Yes. All training zones are climate-controlled for your comfort and safety.

10. Are lockers available?

Yes. Day-use lockers are provided. Bring your own padlock. Please empty lockers before closing hours.

11. Do you provide towels and water?

Towels are available on a rental basis (₹10 per towel). Please carry your own bottle — filtered drinking water is available for refills.

12. Can I bring my own trainer?

For safety and insurance reasons, only FlexFit-certified trainers are allowed to train members inside the facility.

13. Are supplements sold at the gym?

Yes. Our health bar stocks certified whey protein, BCAA, energy drinks, and protein bars. Speak with our nutritionist before purchasing.

14. Do you offer nutrition plans?

Yes. Members get a free basic nutrition consultation upon joining. Custom diet plans are available with our in-house nutritionist.

15. Is there a cancellation or refund policy?

Memberships are non-refundable, but you can transfer them to another person for a nominal fee. Please refer to the policy document for full terms.

16. What safety measures are in place?

All equipment is sanitized daily. Trainers are CPR-certified, and first-aid kits are available at all zones. Emergency exits are clearly marked.

17. Can I use my phone in the gym?

Phones are allowed for music or tracking workouts but must be used respectfully. Photography or video recording requires permission from management.

18. How do I contact support?

- Call: 1800-123-456  
- Email: support@flexfitgym.in  
- Or use the in-app chat support.

19. Do you offer discounts?

Yes. We run seasonal discounts, referral bonuses, and student offers. Check the app or bulletin board for current promotions.

20. What is the minimum age for joining?

You must be at least 16 years old. Teenagers aged 16–17 require parental consent and a beginner orientation session.

Thank you for choosing FlexFit Gym. We’re committed to helping you feel strong, healthy, and confident!
