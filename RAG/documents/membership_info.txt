Welcome to FlexFit Gym! We believe in fitness for everyone, and we offer flexible membership options tailored to your needs and goals.

Our membership plans:

1. Basic Plan – ₹999/month
This plan provides full access to all cardio and strength training equipment. It’s ideal for beginners or anyone looking for a no-frills workout. Access is allowed during non-peak hours only (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on weekdays).

2. Standard Plan – ₹1499/month
In addition to all gym equipment access, this plan includes group classes such as Zumba, HIIT, and Power Yoga. You also receive access to weekend workout slots and free monthly fitness assessments by our in-house trainers. Ideal for people looking to stay consistent and enjoy variety in their workouts.

3. Premium Plan – ₹1999/month
The Premium Plan includes everything in the Standard Plan, plus:
- Personalized training sessions (2 per month)
- One-on-one diet consultation (1 per month)
- Early morning and late-night access (5:00 AM – 10:00 PM)
- Priority bookings for fitness workshops and boot camps

Member Onboarding:
After signing up, every member is given a fitness assessment to evaluate their current fitness level. Based on this assessment, trainers recommend a custom workout and diet plan (available for Standard and Premium plans). The onboarding session includes a gym tour, machine tutorials, and safety guidance.

Discounts and Offers:
- Get 10% off on 6-month plans and 20% off on 12-month plans.
- Corporate discounts available on group sign-ups (minimum 5 people).
- Student discounts available with a valid ID.

Additional Services:
- Personal Training Sessions: ₹500/session (for Basic and Standard members)
- Locker Rental: ₹100/month
- Diet Consultations: ₹300/session (free for Premium)

Membership Freeze:
You can freeze your membership for medical or travel reasons up to 2 months per year. Please inform the front desk at least 7 days in advance.

Refund Policy:
Membership fees are non-refundable except in cases of medical emergencies. All requests must be submitted in writing with valid documentation.

Thank you for choosing FlexFit Gym – where your fitness journey begins!
