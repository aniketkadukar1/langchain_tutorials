FlexFit Gym – General Rules and Guidelines

To ensure a safe, clean, and positive environment for all members and staff, please follow the rules outlined below. Your cooperation helps us maintain the high standards our community values.

General Conduct:

1. All members must check in at the front desk using their membership card or app QR code.
2. Respect fellow gym members. Harassment, intimidation, or inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.
3. Use of abusive language, loud yelling, or slamming weights is discouraged. Maintain a positive and respectful workout space.
4. Children under 16 are not allowed inside the workout areas unless enrolled in special teen fitness programs and accompanied by a trainer.

Cleanliness & Hygiene:

1. Carry a personal towel and use it while working out — especially on benches and mats.
2. Wipe down machines, benches, and equipment with the provided sanitizer after use.
3. Personal hygiene must be maintained. Members with offensive body odor may be asked to leave and return when properly groomed.
4. Gym uniforms or proper activewear must be worn. No jeans, sandals, or open footwear allowed.

Equipment Use:

1. Re-rack weights after every use. Leaving dumbbells or plates on the floor is unsafe.
2. Do not monopolize machines. If others are waiting, limit your sets and allow turns between sets.
3. Report any broken equipment to staff immediately. Do not attempt to fix or tamper with gym equipment.
4. Chalk is not allowed unless permitted by trainers during powerlifting sessions.

Locker Room Rules:

1. Lockers are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Bring your own padlock.
2. Day-use lockers must be cleared before closing hours. Items left overnight will be removed.
3. Keep the washrooms clean. Avoid long showers during peak hours.
4. The gym is not responsible for lost or stolen items. Please keep valuables at home.

Class Etiquette:

1. Arrive 5-10 minutes early for group classes to secure a spot and avoid disruption.
2. If you're more than 10 minutes late, the instructor may ask you to join the next session instead.
3. Turn off mobile phones or put them on silent during classes.
4. Bring your own yoga mat if required, especially during peak hours.

Timings:

- Weekdays: 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Weekends: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Closed on major national holidays (notifications will be sent via app/email)

Emergency Protocols:

1. In case of injury or discomfort, contact the front desk or nearest staff member immediately.
2. Emergency exits are marked and must be kept clear at all times.
3. Fire drills will be conducted once every 3 months. Participation is mandatory.
